💸 SpendSense — AI-Powered Goal-Based Budgeting Assistant
SpendSense is a web-based personal finance app that helps users develop conscious and intentional spending habits through goal-based budgeting and real-time AI-driven feedback.
Unlike traditional budgeting apps that automatically categorize expenses, SpendSense encourages users to actively assign each transaction to a self-defined spending goal at the moment of spending. This increases financial awareness and accountability by making users consciously reflect on where their money is going.
---
✨ Features
Custom spending goals — Create goals like Food, Makeup, or Hobbies with your own monthly limit
Manual transaction logging — Assign each transaction to a goal with automatic date & time stamping
Real-time AI roasts — When you exceed a budget, Claude AI generates a witty, personalized reality check
AI Financial Health Score — Get an overall spending analysis and personalized tips powered by AI
Multi-currency support — USD, KRW, INR, EUR, GBP, JPY, AUD, CAD, CHF
4 themes — Classic Light, Pastel Pink 🌸, Dark Mode 🌙, Dark Pastel 🌙🌸
Spending chart — Visual doughnut chart showing spending distribution across goals
Monthly summary — Progress bars per goal showing how close you are to the limit
Personalized experience — Set your name; the app greets you and uses it in AI feedback
Persistent storage — All data saved locally, nothing lost on refresh
---
🧠 Design Philosophy
SpendSense is built on principles from behavioral psychology and human-centered design. The core idea is that conscious, manual assignment of transactions creates more financial accountability than automatic categorization. When users must actively decide which goal a purchase belongs to, they reflect on their spending in real time — before habits solidify.
The AI feedback system is designed to be emotionally engaging and relatable, especially for students and young adults who respond better to humor than to traditional financial warnings.

🚀 How to Run
Clone or download this repository
Open `index.html` in any modern browser
Click ⚙️ to set your name, theme, and currency
Create a spending goal and start logging transactions
> **Note:** AI features require an internet connection to call the Claude API.
